# The demo video (source)

The 2-minute demo video is a HyperFrames composition: `index.html` is generated by `build_full.py`, with every cut snapped to the beat grid in `beats12.json`. It joins a title, an iMessage and website side-by-side scene, a narrated walkthrough, and an outro.

The recordings (screen recordings of the website and an iPhone), the music and the rendered video are not in this repository: they are large, and the iPhone recording shows a real phone's messages. To rebuild, put your own recordings in `assets/media/` (`site.mp4`, `phone.mp4`, `walk.mp4`) and a music track in `assets/music/`, run `python3 build_full.py`, then `npx hyperframes check` and `npx hyperframes render`. The offsets inside the recordings are set in `build_full.py`.
